## Chapter IV — Her Constellation

- **Books** — She finishes a book and carries it around for days afterwards. Ask her about the ending — she has a whole argument ready.
- **Dreams** — She is ambitious in a quiet way. She would rather do the thing properly than tell you she is doing it.
- **Kindness** — She has stayed gentle in a world that rewards the opposite, and I do not think she knows how rare that is.
- **Jokes** — The private jokes that make no sense to anyone else. Half of them are one word long by now.
- **Songs** — The songs she puts on when she works, and the completely different ones for when she does not want to think.
- **Art** — She sees beauty others walk past. She turns feeling into something the world can hold.
