Chapter One

The Letter

written late,
rewritten twice

Sara,

I've started this more times than I'll admit. Every version sounded like something you'd read on a card, and you'd have seen through it immediately — you always do.

So here is the plain version. I like who I am when I'm around you. That's the whole thing. Not a feeling that arrives in a rush and leaves the same way, but something steadier: I think more carefully, I notice more, I want to be better at the things I'm careless about. You did that without ever asking me to.

you notice things
other people walk
straight past

I love that you make things. That you'll look at something for a long time before you draw it. That you get quiet when you're working and then apologise for being quiet, which you never need to do.

I made this because you deserve something that took time. Not a message that disappears up a screen — something with pages, that stays where you left it, that you can come back to on a bad Tuesday.

It isn't finished. I don't think it's meant to be.

— Bilal

Chapter Five

Fireheart

There are people who survive by becoming harder.

You have somehow remained gentle without ever becoming fragile.

I don't think you know how rare that is. It would have been easier to close, and you didn't. You kept making things. You kept paying attention. You stayed soft in the places most people armour first.

and you'd have found the flame anyway —
you always do

There are still pages missing.
I hope there always will be.

Bilal
